Our Emergency Response Promise

We treat Emergency Cover as a priority service. We aim to respond to all enquiries within 2–4 hours during office hours, and within 12 hours for out-of-hours messages. For urgent requirements, call us directly on 020 37 57 27 67 and our operations team can rapidly deploy officers at short notice.

FAQs

Common Questions

How quickly can you deploy security?

For pre-planned requirements we typically need 48–72 hours' notice. For Emergency Cover, contact us directly and we can urgently deploy available officers at short notice, often within hours.

Are all your operatives licensed?

Yes. Every operative deployed by Spike Security holds a valid, in-date security licence. We verify all licences before deployment and maintain real-time records.

Do you offer flexible contract terms?

Absolutely. We offer short-notice one-off cover, rolling monthly agreements and long-term contracts. We'll find a commercial arrangement that suits your business.

What areas do you cover?

Our primary coverage area is the North West. We regularly deploy across Manchester, Salford, Trafford, Stockport, Bolton, Bury and surrounding areas.
