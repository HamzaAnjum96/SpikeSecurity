FAQs

Common Questions

How quickly can you deploy security?

For pre-planned requirements we typically need 48–72 hours' notice. For emergency cover, contact us directly and we'll do our best to mobilise within hours.

Are all your operatives licensed?

Yes. Every operative deployed by Spike Security holds a valid, in-date security licence. We verify all licences before deployment and maintain real-time records.

Do you offer flexible contract terms?

Absolutely. We offer short-notice one-off cover, rolling monthly agreements and long-term contracts. We'll find a commercial arrangement that suits your business.

What areas do you cover?

Our primary coverage area is Greater Manchester and the North West. We regularly deploy across Manchester, Salford, Trafford, Stockport, Bolton, Bury and surrounding areas.
