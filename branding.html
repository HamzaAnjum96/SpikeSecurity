01Logo

Logo: One shield. Drawn once.

An S cut through a shield. It is the one part of the brand that never changes: always use these files, never redraw, recolour or rebuild it.

Which file do I need?

  • Word, PowerPoint and anything printed on white paperFull colour, light · PNG
  • Websites, slides and posts with a dark backgroundFull colour, dark · PNG
  • Profile pictures and app iconsShield on obsidian · PNG
  • Printers, sign makers and uniform suppliersBrand pack · ZIP · 2.9 MB
Colour
Spike Orange on dark and light backgrounds. Use a one-colour version only when orange can’t be printed or the background is orange.
Clear space
Leave at least x on every side, where x is a quarter of the shield’s height. Nothing else enters that space.
Minimum size
Shield: 16 px on screen, 6 mm in print. Horizontal logo: 120 px or 30 mm wide.
Wordmark
SPIKE SECURITY in Archivo Condensed ExtraBold. It is supplied as artwork. Never retype it.

03Type

Type: Archivo for words. Martian Mono for the numbers.

Archivo does the talking: condensed for signs and headlines, semi-condensed for subheads, normal for reading, labels and buttons. Martian Mono keeps the record: the phone number, times, references and licence numbers, in a face built for code, with a slashed zero that can’t be misread as an O. Both are free under the SIL Open Font License.

04Supergraphic

Supergraphic: The S, up close.

A deep crop of the shield gives banners, covers and signs energy without adding a second shape to the brand.

  1. 01

    Crop deep: at least three times the size of any logo on the same surface.

  2. 02

    Keep a curve and the diagonal in frame, so the S stays recognisable.

  3. 03

    One crop per surface. Never set text over Signal orange.

  4. 04

    The full logo still appears. The crop never replaces it.

05Voice

Voice: Calm. Visible. Accountable.

We write the way a good officer works: clearly, without drama, and with our name on it.

Calm

We explain what will happen and when. We never use fear to win work.

Visible

Say what we do and where we do it in the first line, in words anyone can read at a glance.

Accountable

Back every claim with something a client can check: a licence, a time, a name or a number.

06In use

In use: Built for the night shift.

Artwork for the places the brand actually lives: on uniforms, on site hoardings and on social profiles.

Uniform

Back print 280 mm wide, centred, 80 mm below the collar seam. Chest: horizontal logo, 80 mm wide, left side. Inks: Pantone Orange 021 C, and white for bone.

Site signs

Print-ready A3 PDFs with 3 mm bleed for print shops. They scale cleanly to A2 and A1 for hoardings and gates.

If the client runs the CCTV system, add their name and contact details under the sign. The ICO expects signs to say who is responsible for the cameras.

Social profiles

The same three images everywhere, so every profile reads as one company.

Photography

Real officers on real sites, or no photo at all.

  • Our own peopleSpike officers, photographed with their consent, on sites we actually cover. No stock models.
  • Working, not posingChecking passes, briefing a team, walking a site. No folded arms or posed stares.
  • Real lightDusk, streetlights and venue lighting. Orange appears because it is there, on a tabard or a sign, never as a colour filter.
  • Privacy firstNo identifiable members of the public, no readable licence numbers, and no client sites without written permission.

Install it

Outlook (new) and Outlook on the web

Settings › Account › Signatures. Create a new signature, paste, and save. Choose it for new messages and replies.

Outlook for Windows (classic)

File › Options › Mail › Signatures. Select New, paste into the editor, then OK.

Gmail

Settings › See all settings › General › Signature. Create new, paste, then Save changes at the bottom of the page.

Apple Mail on Mac

Mail › Settings › Signatures. Add one with +, untick “Always match my default message font”, then paste.

iPhone and iPad

Settings › Mail › Signature (on newer versions, Settings › Apps › Mail). Paste. If the styling drops, use the plain-text version.
