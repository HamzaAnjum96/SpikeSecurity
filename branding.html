01 Logo

One shield. Drawn once.

An S cut through a shield. It is the one part of the brand that never changes: always use these files, never redraw, recolour or rebuild it.

Colour
Spike Orange on dark and light backgrounds. Use a one-colour version only when orange can't be printed or the background is orange.
Clear space
Leave at least x on every side, where x is a quarter of the shield's height. Nothing else enters that space.
Minimum size
Shield: 16 px on screen, 6 mm in print. Horizontal lockup: 120 px or 30 mm wide.
Wordmark
SPIKE SECURITY in Archivo Condensed ExtraBold. It is supplied as artwork. Never retype it.

Three lockups

Five colourways

Pick the row that matches your background. SVG and PDF are vector, for print and signs. PNG is for documents, slides and the web.

Full colour, dark

Orange shield, bone name. The primary logo for every dark surface.

Full colour, light

Orange shield, obsidian name. For white paper, bone and pale photos.

Orange

One colour. Embroidery and single-colour print on dark garments.

Bone

One colour. Photography, etching and dark vehicles.

Obsidian

One colour. Hi-vis, Spike Orange panels and print on light stock.

Space and size

120 px · 30 mm

16 px · 6 mm

Shown at actual size. Smaller than this, the S fills in and the name blurs.

Never

  • Recolour the shield.

  • Stretch or squash it.

  • Rotate or tilt it.

  • Add shadows, glows or outlines.

  • Spike Security

    Retype the name in another font.

  • Put the orange shield on orange. Use obsidian.

04 Supergraphic

The S, up close.

A deep crop of the shield gives banners, covers and vehicles energy without adding a second shape to the brand.

  1. 01

    Crop deep: at least three times the size of any logo on the same surface.

  2. 02

    Keep a curve and the diagonal in frame, so the S stays recognisable.

  3. 03

    One crop per surface. Never set text over Signal orange.

  4. 04

    The full logo still appears. The crop never replaces it.

05 Voice

Calm. Visible. Accountable.

We write the way a good officer works: clearly, without drama, and with our name on it.

Calm

We de-escalate. No alarm, no fear, no exclamation marks.

Visible

Easy to find, easy to read. Lead with what we do and where.

Accountable

Specifics we can stand behind: licences, times, names and numbers.

06 In use

Built for the night shift.

Artwork for the places the brand actually lives: on uniforms, on site hoardings and on social profiles.

Uniform

Back print 280 mm wide, centred, 80 mm below the collar seam. Chest: horizontal lockup, 80 mm wide, left side.

A3 print-ready PDFs. They scale cleanly to A2 and A1 for hoardings and gates.

The shield loads from spikesecurity.co.uk, so the signature stays small and works in Gmail, which strips embedded images.

Install it

Outlook (new) and Outlook on the web

Settings › Account › Signatures. Create a new signature, paste, and save. Choose it for new messages and replies.

Outlook for Windows (classic)

File › Options › Mail › Signatures. Select New, paste into the editor, then OK.

Gmail

Settings › See all settings › General › Signature. Create new, paste, then Save changes at the bottom of the page.

Apple Mail on Mac

Mail › Settings › Signatures. Add one with +, untick "Always match my default message font", then paste.

iPhone and iPad

Settings › Mail › Signature (on newer versions, Settings › Apps › Mail). Paste. If the styling drops, use the plain-text version.
