About Spike Security

Built on a foundation of integrity, professionalism and an uncompromising commitment to keeping businesses and people safe.

Who We Are

Manchester-Born. Nationally Trusted.

Spike Security was founded in Manchester in 2025 with a clear mission: to raise the standard of professional security services across the North West. What started as a small team of dedicated operatives has grown into one of Greater Manchester's most respected security companies.

We believe security should be professional, proportionate and people-focused. Every operative we deploy is not just a licence holder — they're a trained professional who represents your business with pride.

Today we protect hundreds of businesses from Spinningfields to Salford Quays, from city-centre venues to out-of-town retail parks. Our reputation is built on reliability, transparency and results.
